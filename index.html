About Me

M. Ikhwan Farhat

A graduate in Pure Mathematics at UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta who combines analytical-logical thinking with creative intuition as a Multimedia Specialist. He possesses comprehensive technical expertise spanning photography, videography, post-production, graphic design, broadcasting, UI/UX, and data analysis.

As the Founder of Trilogi Makna Creative, he has been trusted to produce visual works for 30+ clients across industries, including government agencies, state-owned enterprises, educational institutions, and leading brands. His professional track record includes documentation of large-scale brand activation events such as Polygon and INDOFEST, sports photography, and active involvement in art exhibitions and photography organizations.
